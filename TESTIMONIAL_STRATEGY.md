# Testimonial strategy

Written 2026-10-18, replacing a file that was referenced in `index.html` but
never existed.

## The rule

> **Never publish a quote that is not real.**

Two hidden `<figure class="testimonial">` slots referenced this document from
2026-10-03 onward. Until this file existed they were a promise nobody kept —
which is exactly the slop `DESIGN.md` §7 bans. The slots are now backed by a
process.

## Why the slots are still hidden

A hidden slot costs nothing and promises nothing. An unhidden slot with
placeholder text costs trust. So:

- **One** slot is unhidden when the first real quote lands. Not two.
- A real quote from one person reads as *a person with a reference*.
- Two quotes in the same layout read as *a template*.
- If no quote can be obtained within 30 days, **delete both slots** rather
  than leaving them hidden.

## The ask

One person. Not five.

> I'm putting my name and my work in public and I'd like one honest reference
> on the site — name, role, and one sentence about working with me. If you'd
> rather not, that's completely fine.

Who works: a former colleague, a manager, a client, a Kanmo teammate. Anyone
who can say *"yes, I worked with Akmal — here is what he delivered, and here is
what he was like when it broke."*

## Consent, before publication

Ask for all three, in writing, by email:

1. **Name and role** — e.g. "Rina, Operations Manager, [client anonymised as]
   2025"
2. **The quote, verbatim** — never edited, never shortened without asking
3. **Permission to publish** — including whether the company may be named

If any of the three is withheld, publish without it. An anonymised quote from
a real person still converts; a misattributed one ends the relationship and
the reference.

## What a good quote contains

The most valuable quotes are not the nicest ones.

| Contains | Example |
|---|---|
| A specific thing that was true | "He found Rp 340 juta we'd been writing off as lost sales" |
| A moment of difficulty | "The pipeline failed the week we launched and he'd already told us it would" |
| Evidence of judgement | "He told us not to buy the thing he was being paid to sell us" |

The third is worth more than all testimonials combined. A firm will never
publish it, because it contradicts the sale.

## Never

- Edit a quote for length or tone
- Compose a quote from things someone said in a different context
- Use a quote from someone who has not seen it in this exact form
- Attach a name to an anonymous reference
- Publish a relationship that has ended badly without asking first
