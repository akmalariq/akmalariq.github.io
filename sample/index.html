What it answers

Three questions, every morning

Did we actually make money?

Net sales after refunds and fees, per branch and per channel — not gross, not before the marketplace takes its cut.

Why is the bank different?

Every rupiah that arrived at the bank but is not matched to an order, with the likely reason attached. This is the argument that happens on the 3rd of every month.

What should we reorder, and when?

Months of cover per product against what is actually selling. A spreadsheet sorted by alphabetical order is not a reorder list.
