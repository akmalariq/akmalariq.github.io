Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy covers akmalariq.dev and the apps hosted on it, including Dompet (a personal money manager at akmalariq.dev/projects/dompet). It explains what data is collected, how it is used, and how you can have it removed. It is a small, personal project run by Akmal Ariq — there is no advertising, and your data is never sold.

What is collected

  • Account basics from Google Sign-In. When you sign in with Google you share your name, email address and profile picture. We use this only to identify your account. We do not receive your password and cannot access your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Data you enter. The transactions, amounts, categories, accounts, budgets and notes you add to Dompet, and the email address (and optional name) you submit to the waiting list.
  • Basic technical data. Standard request information processed by our hosting provider to serve the site and keep it secure.

How it is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app: to show your dashboard, budgets and reports, to keep your account secure, and to email you about your waiting-list invite. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app, and it is not sold or rented.

Shared Dompets

Dompet lets you share a ledger with other people. If you create or join a shared Dompet, its accounts, categories, transactions and budgets are visible to every member of that Dompet, and each entry shows who added it. If you leave a shared Dompet your access is removed immediately, but entries you added stay visible to the remaining members. The owner can remove members at any time.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Cloudflare — hosting for the site and app, and the database (D1) that stores your data.
  • Google — sign-in only.
  • Resend — sending waiting-list and invite emails.

Your data is not shared with anyone else, except where required by law.

Cookies and sessions

Signing in sets a single secure, HTTP-only session cookie so you stay logged in. No tracking, analytics or advertising cookies are used.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and everything in it, email akmalariqs@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about your data? Email akmalariqs@gmail.com.
