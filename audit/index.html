What arrives

Six pages, and one number that matters

The variance, to the rupiah

Every rupiah that reached the bank and did not match an order, split by cause: fees, refunds in transit, settlement lag, and the ones nobody can explain. The unexplained ones are named, not averaged away.

What it costs per month

The variance, annualised, expressed in your margin. Not "data quality issues" — a figure your partner can argue with.

What is missing that you cannot see

Questions your data cannot answer yet, ranked by what they would be worth if you could. This is usually the most valuable page in the report.

What I would fix first

One recommendation, with the reasoning. Often something you can do yourself this week with no software at all.

When this is the wrong purchase

Nothing is recorded anywhere

If transactions live in someone's head or in a notebook, there is nothing to reconcile and the audit becomes research. That is a different engagement and a worse one. Wait until you are recording.

You can hire an analyst

Then hire one. You will get more out of a full-time hire than from a five-day report, and I will say so on the call rather than take the money.

You want a dashboard, not a finding

If what you need is a screen, not an answer, this is the wrong product. The build is designed for the answer first.

Nobody is named when the numbers are wrong

If a discrepancy has never once caused an awkward conversation, fixing it has no value and you will not use the report.

Before you ask

Questions I already know

Is the 2.5 million credited back, or is that a trick?

Credited in full against the Rp 7.500.000 build if you continue within 30 days of delivery. It is arithmetic, not a discount: you are not paying twice for the same month of analysis. If you do not continue, you keep the report and the credit simply expires.

What if my data turns out to be fine?

Then the report says so, in writing, and you have spent 2.5 million to avoid a 7.5 million mistake. This happens. Several of the audits I have described in my own outreach were run precisely because I suspected the answer might be "nothing is wrong" — that is a legitimate outcome, not a failure.

Will my competitors see my numbers?

No. The report is yours. I use anonymised, aggregate patterns when I write case studies, and only with written permission naming what may be published. Blank the names yourself if you prefer — see the email template above.

Five days for three exports of different shapes?

Five working days from the moment I have all three. If they are in formats I cannot read, I will tell you on day one and the clock does not start until it is readable. I would rather say that than miss a date quietly.

Is 2.5 million just a deposit on the real work?

No. The audit is complete on its own. It has an end, six pages, and a recommendation you may act on by yourself. The build is a separate decision you make afterwards with the information in front of you.

Do I need to talk to you at all?

No. The whole flow is email. I will ask for the period and the exports, then deliver. If you want a call afterwards to walk through it, fine — but it is not a condition of the audit.
